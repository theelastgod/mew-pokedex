# Mew · No. 151

A fan field guide to Mew, the New Species Pokémon. The ancestor, the mirage, the empty truck, and the clone made from one cell.

## Live

https://mew-pokedex.pages.dev

## What this tells

- Mew is Pokémon No. 151, a Psychic-type Mythical Pokémon.
- Its genes are said to contain every other Pokémon, which is why it can learn nearly every move.
- It was added to Red and Green late, by Shigeki Morimoto, and first printed in CoroCoro in May 1996.
- The truck by the S.S. Anne is a rumor. Mew was never under it.
- A cell from Mew was used to create Mewtwo.
- Faraway Island, reached with the Old Sea Map, is the place the later games actually give.
