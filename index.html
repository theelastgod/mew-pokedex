The truck was empty.

Players decided Mew was underneath the truck parked by the harbor. The truck is scenery. Shigeki Morimoto added Mew in space that was left at the end of Red and Green, and CoroCoro printed the creature in May 1996.

For a long time the only copy was an event Pokémon, carried across a link cable.
